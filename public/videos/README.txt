Sample videos for the AI Video Production page.

Drop your three sample video files here with these exact names:

  sample-1.mp4   (labelled "Corporate Intro")
  sample-2.mp4   (labelled "AI Presenter")
  sample-3.mp4   (labelled "Product Video")

Notes:
- Use .mp4 (H.264) for the widest browser support.
- 16:9 videos fit the player best.
- Optional: add a poster image (e.g. sample-1.jpg) and set `poster`
  in src/components/AiVideo/AiVideoContent.jsx if you want a thumbnail
  before the video loads.

To change a label or add/remove a video, edit the SAMPLES array in
src/components/AiVideo/AiVideoContent.jsx.
